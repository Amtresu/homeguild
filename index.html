The Standard

Seven checks. In order. Every year.

Most directories sell a badge to whoever pays. We don't sell placement. A firm passes each of these in sequence or it doesn't appear — and it re-passes all seven every August. Read the full Standard →

  1. 01

    License & registration

    Verified directly against Idaho DOPL and the Contractor Registration record — not a screenshot from the applicant.

  2. 02

    Insurance on file

    Current general liability and, where crews are employed, workers' compensation certificates held by us, renewed on expiry.

  3. 03

    Three homeowner references, called

    We phone each one. We ask what went wrong, not just what went right, and how it was handled.

  4. 04

    Five years operating in the Valley

    Established here, with a local address and a track record that predates the boom. No pop-up franchises.

  5. 05

    A site visit to finished work

    A Guild reviewer walks a completed job, unannounced to the crew, with the homeowner present.

  6. 06

    Pricing transparency pledge

    Written estimates, itemized change orders, and no member-only "referral fees" hidden in the quote. Ever.

  7. 07

    Annual re-review

    Steps one through six, again, every year. Two substantiated member complaints trigger an immediate review.

We are paid by homeowners, so we answer to homeowners. No firm has ever bought its way onto this page, and none will.

No lead fees, referral fees, or "featured" placement. Firms pay nothing to be listed.
Nomination only. A firm gets reviewed when a current member or a listed firm puts its name forward.
Removals are published. When a firm leaves the register, members are told why.
Reviewers live here. Every site visit is done by a Guild reviewer based in the Treasure Valley.
