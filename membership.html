Questions

The ones we get most.

Why does a homeowner pay and a firm doesn't?
Because whoever pays is who the list serves. Every other directory in the Valley is paid by contractors, so it exists to send contractors leads. We're paid by homeowners, so we exist to tell homeowners the truth.
Is $180 a year worth it?
One avoided bad hire on a $6,000 job pays for it many times over. But the honest answer is that most members use the register two or three times a year and keep it for the removal notices — knowing when a firm they've used has stopped meeting the Standard.
Can I rent out my home and still join?
Membership is for owner-occupied homes. Landlords and property managers have a different set of incentives and a different set of firms; we may build a register for them later, but not by diluting this one.
What if a listed firm does a bad job for me?
Tell us. A reviewer calls you and the firm within a week. Two substantiated complaints in twelve months trigger an immediate re-review; one substantiated pricing complaint removes the firm outright.
I run a good firm. Why can't I just apply?
Because a form is the easiest thing in the world to fill in. A nomination from someone who has paid you and would do it again is not. Ask a customer who is a member — or ask a listed firm you've worked alongside.
Do you cover Nampa and Caldwell as well as Boise?
Yes — Ada and Canyon counties. Reviewers live on both sides of the county line, and the register is filtered by town so you can see who actually works near you.
