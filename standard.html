The Standard · Revised August 2026

Seven checks. In order. Every year.

This is the whole test. A firm passes each step in sequence or it does not appear on the register. There is no partial credit, no probationary listing, and no fee that changes the outcome. What follows is what we check, how we check it, and what fails a firm.

  1. 01

    License & registration

    The firm holds every Idaho license and registration its trade requires, in its own name, current as of the review date.

    How we check. A reviewer pulls the record directly from the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses and the Contractor Registration database. We never accept a document supplied by the applicant.
    What we record. License numbers, issue and expiry dates, and any disciplinary history, entered in the firm's file.
    Fails on: an expired credential, a license held by a person no longer with the firm, or any open disciplinary action.
  2. 02

    Insurance on file

    General liability of at least $1M per occurrence, and workers' compensation wherever the firm employs a crew.

    How we check. The certificate of insurance is issued to the Guild by the carrier, naming us as certificate holder, so we are notified on cancellation.
    What we record. Carrier, policy numbers, limits, and expiry. Renewals are chased thirty days out.
    Fails on: a lapsed policy at any point during the year, or a crew working without comp coverage.
  3. 03

    Three homeowner references, called

    Three recent residential customers, none related to the firm, at least one from a job over $5,000.

    How we check. A reviewer phones each reference and works through the same twelve questions. The questions that matter most: what went wrong, how it was handled, and what the final bill was against the estimate.
    What we record. A one-paragraph summary of each call, visible to members on the firm's page.
    Fails on: an unreachable reference, an unresolved dispute, or a final bill more than 15% over a written estimate without a signed change order.
  4. 04

    Five years operating in the Valley

    Continuous operation in Ada or Canyon County for at least five years under the same ownership.

    How we check. Secretary of State filing date, the earliest license record, and a local business address that is not a mailbox.
    Why five. The Valley's growth brought a wave of firms that arrived in a boom and leave in a slowdown. Five years covers one full cycle.
    Fails on: an ownership change inside the window, an out-of-state parent that controls scheduling and pricing, or a franchise less than five years in the market.
  5. 05

    A site visit to finished work

    A Guild reviewer walks a completed job, chosen by us from the reference list, with the homeowner present.

    How we check. We pick the job. The firm is told the week, not the day. The reviewer looks at what a homeowner can't see from the driveway: flashing, terminations, panel labeling, drainage, cleanup.
    What we record. A short written report with photographs, shared with the firm and visible to members.
    Fails on: any work that would not pass the relevant inspection, or a homeowner who declines to host the visit.
  6. 06

    Pricing transparency pledge

    Written estimates, itemized change orders signed before the work, and no fee of any kind paid to the Guild or to anyone for the referral.

    How we check. The firm signs the pledge annually. Reference calls and member reports test whether it is kept.
    What it rules out. Lead fees, "preferred vendor" fees, kickbacks to property managers or agents, and member-only pricing that is really a surcharge.
    Fails on: a single substantiated instance. This is the check most likely to remove a firm mid-year.
  7. 07

    Annual re-review

    Steps one through six again, every August, for every firm on the register.

    How we check. Same process, new references, a new site visit. Nothing carries over from the prior year except the file.
    Between cycles. Two substantiated member complaints in twelve months trigger an immediate review. Removals are published to members with the reason.
    Fails on: any step failed. A removed firm may be re-nominated after twelve months.
Who does the checking

Reviewers live here and don't take work from firms.

Guild reviewers are Treasure Valley residents with a trade background — a retired inspector, a former GC, a journeyman electrician. None may hold an interest in a listed firm or accept work from one. Every site-visit report carries the reviewer's name.
