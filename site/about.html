About the Guild

A list for the people who live here.

The Treasure Valley added a hundred thousand people in a decade. The firms that serve them arrived just as fast, and the directories that rank those firms are paid by the firms. The HomeGuild started in 2024 as a shared spreadsheet between neighbors on the Bench. It's still that, with a Standard attached.

Why it exists

Every other list is paid for by the people on it.

Lead-generation directories charge contractors per inquiry. Review sites sell "featured" placement. Neighborhood apps bury honest recommendations under sponsored ones. None of them will ever phone your references or walk your finished job.

So we did the thing nobody else would pay for: reviewers who live here, a Standard that doesn't bend, and a fee that comes from homeowners so the incentive points the right way.

Reviewers

Trade people, not salespeople.

Every reviewer has worked in a trade or inspected one. None may hold an interest in a listed firm, accept work from one, or review a firm they've hired. Their name goes on every report.

Bylaws, in brief

The rules we can't change quietly.

The full bylaws are available to members. These are the clauses that protect the register from us.

§1No firm may pay the Guild, a reviewer, or a member anything for listing, placement, referral, or review.
§2Every step of the Standard is checked by a reviewer, never self-certified by the firm.
§3Removals are published to members with the reason, in the same words the firm received.
§4Per-trade caps may only be raised by a vote of members, not by the Guild.
§5Any change to the Standard is announced to members ninety days before it takes effect.
§6Member addresses are never shared with listed firms. Contact runs member → firm, not the reverse.
